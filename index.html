Yellowskill Channel Manager

Internal tool of Nana Company

Yellowskill Channel Manager is an internal tool that Nana Company uses to manage our own YouTube channel, 노란스킬 (Yellowskill), which teaches memorization techniques for the Korean licensed real estate agent and housing manager exams.

What it does

  • Uploads YouTube Shorts cut from our own lecture videos, with title, description, tags and schedule
  • Updates metadata and captions of our own videos
  • Reads analytics and membership levels of our own channel to plan content

The tool uses YouTube API Services. It is used only by our team, is not offered to the public, and never accesses other channels' data.
